I'm Zoë, a relationship coach. I work with couples, and with people on their own, who are tired of the same old arguments and want to feel close again.

Are you ready to do the work?

Good relationships take effort. Mostly that means being honest about your own part, owning it, and getting curious about your partner instead of keeping score.

  • Better relationships begin with you.
  • Love is a skill. Connection is a practice.

I know what it means
to love imperfectly.

Fourteen years ago, I packed up my life in the UK, married my holiday romance and started a whole new adventure in Canada. Romantic? Absolutely. A little bonkers? Definitely. But here I am, a wife, a mum to two daughters, and I wouldn't change a thing.

My understanding of relationships didn't come from a textbook. It came from living one. The long-distance years, the compromises, the beautiful chaos of raising children, the inevitable bumps in the road, and learning what it really means to keep choosing each other, especially when it's hard.

I'm a certified relationship coach with Level 1 training in Relational Life Therapy (RLT). I believe in warmth, compassion and telling the truth, even when it's uncomfortable. I won't tiptoe around the hard stuff, and I won't just tell you what you want to hear. I'll gently challenge you, call you forward and help you recognize the patterns that might be standing between you and the connection you crave.

Because real change takes courage. It means getting curious about yourself, owning your part and being willing to do things differently.

Together, we'll dig a little deeper, challenge some old stories and make room for more honesty, intimacy and love. Not a perfect relationship, but a more connected, fulfilling and authentic one.

Questions you
might have

Is coaching the same as therapy?

No. Therapy can treat mental health conditions and work through trauma. Coaching looks at how you relate to each other now, and what you'll do differently from here. If something comes up that needs a therapist, such as abuse, addiction or trauma, I'll tell you and help you find one.

Do we meet online?

Yes. Sessions are on video, so you can join from home, anywhere in Canada. All you need is a quiet room and a laptop or phone.

What if my partner won't come?

You can start on your own. When you change your side of a pattern, the whole pattern starts to shift, and partners often get curious and join later. Individual coaching is a good place to begin.

What happens on the free call?

We'll spend 30 minutes talking about what's going on and what you'd like to be different. I'll tell you honestly whether I think I can help and how we'd work together. No pressure and no hard sell.

Is what I share kept private?

Yes. What you tell me stays between us, unless I believe someone is at risk of serious harm. I'll go through this with you before we start.

What if I need to cancel?

Life happens. Give me 48 hours' notice and we'll find another time. Sessions cancelled with less notice count as used. Packages are valid for six months.
